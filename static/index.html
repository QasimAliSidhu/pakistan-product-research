Decisions you can trace

The tool searches public marketplace and supplier pages with OpenSERP and DDGS. It extracts product JSON-LD and Daraz’s structured listing data when accessible. It respects robots.txt and reports blocked or missing data. No login or CAPTCHA bypass is used.

Sales and demand

Five years of exact Daraz product sales are not available from the public sources integrated here. Import your seller reports or licensed historical data for sales analysis. Trends are collected for Pakistan and aggregated to monthly means. Independently normalized keywords cannot compare absolute demand. Forecasts require 24 consecutive completed months and show the actual coverage.

Forecasting

Recent mean, damped trend and seasonal naive models compete on rolling one-month holdouts from the last 6–12 months. The lowest mean absolute error wins. The next 12 months include widening residual-based scenario ranges. These ranges are not calibrated confidence intervals, and five-year search data does not make a sales forecast reliable.

Profit and cash

Landed cost = purchase + inbound freight + duties. Platform deductions = commission + payment fee + tax on those two fees + withholding + fixed fee. Expected cash contribution = (1 − return rate) × successful-order contribution − return rate × loss per return − advertising per placed order. Returned stock is assumed recoverable except for the loss you enter. Withholding is treated as a cash deduction; it may differ from final tax expense.

Startup cash includes initial stock, batch fulfilment and advertising, a conservative replenishment reserve for supplier lead time plus payout delay, setup, one month of fixed costs and contingency. Monthly profit deducts fixed costs. All fee bases are adjustable assumptions; validate the model against your seller settlement statement.
